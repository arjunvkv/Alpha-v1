# BOOK I: [O] Order Flow, Market Microstructure & Liquidity Dynamics

`
                           THE CONTINUOUS ORDER BOOK
          ASKS (Sell Limits)      ───  Passive Liquidity (Resistance)
          ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
          SPREAD (Bid-Ask Gap)    ───  Crossing Cost (.35 - .50)
          ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
          BIDS (Buy Limits)       ───  Passive Liquidity (Support)

      AGGRESSIVE MARKET ORDERS    ───  Consume Liquidity (Demand Urgency)
      PASSIVE LIMIT ORDERS        ───  Provide Liquidity (Absorb Pressure)
`

### 1. Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) Mechanics
In electronic order books, every transaction consists of an **Aggressive Order** (taking liquidity across the spread) interacting with a **Passive Order** (providing resting liquidity on the book).
  Volume Delta = Volume traded at the Ask (Aggressive Buyers) - Volume traded at the Bid (Aggressive Sellers)
  CVD_t = Sum of Delta_i from i=1 to t

#### Order Flow Regimes:
* **Positive CVD + Rising Price (Initiative Buying)**: Aggressive buyers are lifting offers faster than sellers can refresh liquidity. Validates trend continuation.
* **Negative CVD + Falling Price (Initiative Selling)**: Aggressive sellers are hitting bids with high velocity. Validates downward impulse.
* **CVD Divergence / Passive Absorption**:
  * *Bullish Absorption*: Price falls into a structural level (Demand FVG CE or VAL) while Delta prints heavily negative, yet price refuses to make lower lows. Passive institutional buy limits are soaking up all aggressive sell market orders.
  * *Bearish Absorption*: Price rallies into structural resistance (Supply FVG CE or VAH) with heavily positive Delta, but price stalls. Passive institutional sell limits are absorbing aggressive buyers.

### 2. Tick Velocity (t/m) and Microstructure Posture
Tick velocity measures the intensity of market participation (ticks arriving per minute on M1):
  Velocity = Tick Count / Delta t (Minutes)

| Velocity Band | Market Regime | Execution Protocol |
|---|---|---|
| **Quiet Tape (<40 t/m)** | Low institutional participation; consolidation. | Single probing limit (0.5 lot) or patience. |
| **Normal Velocity (40 - 120 t/m)** | Regular two-way institutional auction. | Standard structural bracket staging (1.0 lot). |
| **Institutional Burst (>150 t/m)** | Stop run, news impulse, or session sweep. | Stage 2-tiered resting limits to catch the extreme wick; NEVER chase with market orders. |

### 2. Market Structure Shifts: CHoCH vs. BOS
* **Break of Structure (BOS)**: A continuation signal where price closes with a full candle body beyond the most recent swing high (uptrend) or swing low (downtrend).
* **Change of Character (CHoCH)**: An early reversal signal where price sweeps a key liquidity high/low and aggressively displaces back across the most recent counter-swing point.
* **Displacement Validation**: A true structural shift requires **displacement**—large, high-volume, energetic candles leaving unmitigated Fair Value Gaps in their wake. Small, overlapping, low-volume candles represent chop, not institutional sponsorship.

# BOOK V: [N] Volume Profile, Market Profile & Fair Value Gaps (PD Arrays)

### 1. Volume Profile & Auction Market Theory (AMT)
Volume Profile plots the distribution of traded volume at price rather than time:
* **Point of Control (POC)**: Price level with the absolute highest traded volume. High gravitational pull.
* **Value Area High (VAH)**: Upper boundary containing 70% of volume. Acts as institutional supply/resistance.
* **Value Area Low (VAL)**: Lower boundary containing 70% of volume. Acts as institutional demand/support.

`
       [VAH: 4342.85] ─── Premium / Exhaustion Threshold
             │
       [POC: 4325.35] ─── Fair Value / High Volume Liquidity Core
             │
       [VAL: 4304.67] ─── Discount / Accumulation Threshold
`

#### Jim Dalton's 80% Rule of Value Area Rotation:
If price opens or trades outside the previous session's Value Area, and then prints **two consecutive 15-minute candle closes back INSIDE the Value Area**, there is an **80% statistical probability** that price will rotate entirely across the Value Area to test the opposite boundary.

### 2. Fair Value Gap (FVG) Anatomy & Fill Rate Ontology
An FVG is an inefficiency created when a 3-candle sequence moves so rapidly that Candle 1 and Candle 3 do not overlap:
* **Consequent Encroachment (CE)**: The exact **50% midpoint** of the FVG. This is the primary institutional rebalance anchor.
* **Fill Rate Ontology**:
  * **Fresh (<30% fill)**: Pristine institutional imbalance. Highest reaction probability.
  * **Optimal CE (30% - 60% fill)**: Consequent Encroachment tested. High reaction probability.
  * **Exhausted (>60% fill)**: Imbalance fully mitigated. INSUFFICIENT EDGE — do not trade!
